Privacy

This page explains what Gineve Music (ginevemusic.com) collects and why. Questions: ginevebase@gmail.com.

Booking enquiries

When you send the booking form we receive your name, phone number, email, event details and message. We use them only to reply to you and plan your event. The form is processed by Formspree, which delivers the enquiry to us by email.

Advertising pixel

If you click Accept on the cookie notice, we load the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel. It records which pages you view and when you contact us or send an enquiry, so we can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. Meta may combine this with information it already holds about you. If you click Decline, the Pixel is not loaded.
