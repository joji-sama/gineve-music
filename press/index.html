Biography

Gineve is a Nigerian vocalist and live performer, based in Lagos with a repertoire spanning Jazz, R&B, and Afro-soul, available for events nationwide and internationally. A 2025 participant in the YES (Youth Excellence on Stage) Academy — U.S. Embassy & American Voices — she has built a reputation for reliability, professionalism, and a commanding stage presence across Nigeria’s live music scene.

Through Gineve Music, her premium live entertainment brand, she curates performances for diplomatic residences, corporate galas, luxury private events, intimate lounges, and destination celebrations — trusted by event planners, corporate organizers, and luxury brands to deliver musical excellence that feels both cinematic and intimate.
