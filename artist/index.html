Story

Known as Gineve, Eve Otokpa is a Nigerian vocalist and live performer whose repertoire spans jazz, R&B and Afro-soul.

A YES Academy 2025 participant (U.S. Embassy & American Voices), she brings technical range and stage presence to every performance, from intimate lounges to premium corporate stages. She is also the founder of Gineve Music, Lagos and Abuja’s live entertainment company.
