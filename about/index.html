About

The Story

Gineve — Nigerian jazz & soul vocalist and live performer, based in Lagos with bookings available nationwide and internationally. Founder of Gineve Music.

The Artist

Gineve is a Nigerian vocalist and live performer based in Lagos, with a repertoire spanning jazz and classic soul, R&B, Afro-soul and gospel. A 2025 participant in the YES (Youth Excellence on Stage) Academy — U.S. Embassy & American Voices — she has built a reputation for reliability, professionalism and a commanding stage presence across Nigeria's live music scene, and takes bookings nationwide and internationally.

Through Gineve Music, her premium live entertainment brand, she curates performances for diplomatic residences, corporate galas, luxury private events, intimate lounges and destination celebrations — trusted by event planners, corporate organizers and luxury brands to deliver musical excellence that feels both cinematic and intimate. Every booking is built around the room: the right ensemble, a rehearsed set, and a performance shaped for the evening.

Frequently Asked

Do you travel for events?

Yes — Gineve Music takes bookings anywhere in Nigeria and internationally. Travel and logistics are quoted per event.

What genres do you cover?

Jazz and classic soul, Afro-soul and contemporary, and sacred or ceremonial repertoire — tailored to the event.

Can you provide sound & equipment?

Yes — sound system setup and on-site management are available as an add-on or standalone service.
